ABOUT THE GAME

Meet SNOWY — a tiny cat with a big heart, lost in an endless winter world. Jump across icy platforms, dodge falling icicles, befriend forest spirits, and uncover the secret of the eternal snowfall.

Inspired by classic 8-bit platformers we grew up with, Snowy Cats blends crisp pixel graphics, hand-crafted level design and a chiptune soundtrack into one cozy adventure.

Built by two developers, one cat, and a whole lot of love. No microtransactions. No ads. Just pure platforming joy.
