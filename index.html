ABOUT THE GAME

Meet your cat — a brave little pixel feline scaling endless snowy peaks. Leap over obstacles, collect sparkling coins and unlock an entire wardrobe of adorable hats and costumes!

Snowy Cats is a pixel art endless runner platformer where you guide your cat through beautiful snow-covered mountains. Simple controls, satisfying progress, and a whole lot of charm.

Currently in active development. Coming soon to App Store and Google Play!
